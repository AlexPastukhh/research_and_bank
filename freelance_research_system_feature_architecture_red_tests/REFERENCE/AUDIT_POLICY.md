# Audit policy

AX01–AX26 are persistent axes. Green unit tests do not by themselves imply audit clearance. Release-gate axes require executable evidence. Fixed release-gate findings must become regression tests.


## Golden Paths

AX19, AX21 and AX23 require all release-gate Golden Paths to pass. Golden Paths are end-to-end conformance evidence and complement regression/adversarial tests.
